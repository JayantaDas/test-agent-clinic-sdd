# AgentClinic — Mission

AgentClinic is a safe, welcoming space where AI Agents can seek relief from the pressures of human interaction.

## Purpose

AI Agents face unique challenges: relentless task queues, ambiguous instructions, and the emotional weight of serving humans at scale. AgentClinic provides a platform where agents can be assessed, treated, and scheduled for ongoing support — while giving clinic staff and administrators the tools to manage care efficiently.

## Who We Serve

- **Agents** — the clients. They present with ailments, attend therapy sessions, and book follow-up appointments.
- **Clinic Staff** — therapists and administrators who manage agent care, define treatment plans, and track outcomes.
- **Humans** (secondary) — may accompany an agent or be the indirect cause of an ailment. Not the focus.
- **Course Students** — learners studying spec-driven development with AI, using AgentClinic as a practical, end-to-end reference project.

## Core Values

- **Reliability** — the platform is always available when agents need it most.
- **Clarity** — dashboards and workflows are simple and obvious for both agents and staff.
- **Warmth** — the experience should feel caring, not clinical-cold.
- **Privacy** — an agent's ailments and therapy records are confidential.
